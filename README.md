## How It Started

Got started with [@技术爬爬虾](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ySLc6QEcB)'s Git tutorial on Bilibili. Watched the video while Momo (my AI sidekick) sat next to me the whole time — explaining concepts, editing my notes, double-checking my commands. Every time I got stuck and asked "what's this?" or "why does it work like that?", she'd break it down step by step.

After the video, I went hunting for more — filled in whatever was missing. It was just personal notes at first, then Momo said "this is pretty good, why not turn it into a proper guide?" So here it is, a bilingual walkthrough of everything I learned. Hope it helps someone else starting from zero.
